SECTION 3: PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

3.1 Eligibility Requirements

  For a student to be registered with the Career Development Centre and participate in campus recruitment drives, the following conditions MUST be met:

  Academic Requirements:
  - Minimum CGPA: 6.5 or above calculated across all semesters completed at time of registration (typically end of 7th semester).
  - No standing backlogs (active failed subjects) at the time of placement registration.
  - Minimum 85% attendance in all Career Development Centre (CDC) training sessions conducted from 5th semester onwards.

  Documentation Requirements:
  - Completed Placement Registration Form on the CDC portal (cdc.aitm.edu.in)
  - Updated Resume uploaded in the prescribed AITM resume format (Word/PDF)
  - LinkedIn profile URL (mandatory)
  - GitHub/Portfolio link (mandatory for CSE, IT, AI&DS students)

3.2 CGPA Relaxation Policy

  - Students with CGPA between 6.0 and 6.5 may apply for a relaxation with valid reasons (e.g., health issues, family emergencies) with documentation. Approval is at the discretion of the CDC Director.
  - Students with CGPA below 6.0 are NOT eligible for on-campus placements but may avail CDC resources for off-campus job search support.

3.3 Placement Attempt Rules

  - Each student gets a maximum of THREE job offer acceptance chances.
  - Once a student accepts an offer (signs the offer letter), they are removed from the active placement pool. Exception: if the offer is from a "Dream Company" (CTC > ₹15 LPA), students may continue to participate in Tier 1 drives only.
  - Students must inform CDC within 24 hours of receiving any off-campus offer to update placement records.

SECTION 4: CAREER DEVELOPMENT CENTRE SERVICES

4.1 Training Programs

  The CDC provides the following mandatory and optional training programs:

  Mandatory (5th Semester onwards):
  - Aptitude & Quantitative Reasoning (40 hours, 5th Sem)
  - Communication Skills & Group Discussion (30 hours, 5th Sem)
  - Technical Interview Preparation - DSA & DBMS (60 hours, 6th Sem)
  - Mock Interviews (individual feedback sessions, 7th Sem)

  Optional / Elective (Available to all):
  - AWS Cloud Practitioner Certification Support (free of cost for final year)
  - Microsoft Azure Fundamentals Certification Support
  - GATE Examination Coaching (3 hours/week, 7th & 8th Sem)
  - Entrepreneurship Development Cell workshops

4.2 Internship Policy

  - Students are encouraged to complete at least ONE internship (minimum 4 weeks) during summer vacations (between 2nd and 3rd year).
  - CDC maintains an internship portal with 80+ partner companies.
  - Internships completed through CDC channels are recorded in the student's official transcript.
  - Students with verified internship offers from companies offering PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) get CDC priority in placement registration.

4.3 Higher Education Support

  CDC supports students pursuing higher education after B.Tech:
  - GATE Mentoring: Regular mock tests and guidance for IIT/NIT M.Tech admissions.
  - GRE/GMAT Preparation: Tie-ups with coaching institutes (discounted rates).
  - MBA Preparation: Connections to top B-School alumni for guidance sessions.

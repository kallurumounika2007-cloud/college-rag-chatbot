SECTION 5: ATTENDANCE POLICY

5.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement

  - All students must maintain a MINIMUM of 75% aggregate attendance in each theory course and each laboratory course separately.
  - Students with attendance below 75% but above 65% may apply for Medical Condonation by submitting a medical certificate from a registered physician within 7 days of recovery.
  - Students below 65% attendance in any subject will be DETAINED and are NOT eligible to appear in the End-Semester Examination for that subject. They must re-enroll in the next academic year.

5.2 Attendance Calculation

  Attendance is calculated as: (Classes Attended / Total Classes Held) × 100

  Attendance is computed separately for:
  - Each theory/lecture course
  - Each laboratory course
  - Each tutorial session (counted separately from theory)

5.3 Attendance Monitoring

  - Biometric attendance is recorded for every session using the AITM Smart Attendance System.
  - Students can view real-time attendance on the student portal: portal.aitm.edu.in
  - Attendance SMS alerts are sent to parents/guardians when attendance falls below 80%.
  - Department-level Attendance Committee meetings are held every 15 days to identify defaulters.

5.4 Consequences of Insufficient Attendance

  - Below 80%: First warning letter issued to student and parents.
  - Below 75%: Student is barred from University Examinations unless medical condonation is granted.
  - Below 65%: Student is detained. The year is not counted towards degree completion.

SECTION 6: EXAMINATION RULES & GRADING

6.1 Examination Pattern (Theory Courses)

  Assessment Component                      Weightage
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Unit Test 1 (20 marks, syllabus: units 1-2)   20%
  Unit Test 2 (20 marks, syllabus: units 3-4)   20%
  Mid-Semester Exam (50 marks, units 1-3)       20%
  End-Semester Exam (100 marks, full syllabus)  40%
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Total                                          100%

6.2 Grading System (CGPA Scale: 10 Point)

  Marks Range      Grade    Grade Points
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  90 - 100         O        10
  80 - 89          A+       9
  70 - 79          A        8
  60 - 69          B+       7
  50 - 59          B        6
  40 - 49          C        5
  Below 40         F (Fail) 0

  Minimum passing marks in End-Semester Exam: 40 out of 100 (independently from internal marks).
  A student must pass both internal assessment AND end-semester examination separately.

6.3 Backlog Policy

  - Students who fail in up to 3 subjects are eligible for Supplementary Examinations conducted in January (for Autumn Semester) and July (for Spring Semester).
  - Students with more than 3 backlogs must re-enroll in the failed subjects next year.
  - Maximum allowed backlogs to proceed to the next year: 4 subjects.
  - Students with more than 6 accumulated backlogs may be asked to discontinue or repeat the year.

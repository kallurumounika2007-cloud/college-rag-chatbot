SECTION 1: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR B.TECH ADMISSION

1.1 Academic Qualifications

  All candidates must have passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary Certificate) or equivalent examination from a recognized board with the following minimum requirements:

  General Category:
  - Minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM)
  - No subject-wise minimum requirement beyond the aggregate

  SC / ST / OBC / PwD Category:
  - Minimum 55% aggregate in PCM (5% relaxation from General criteria)

  Lateral Entry (3rd Year Direct Admission for Diploma Holders):
  - Minimum 60% in relevant Diploma program from a State Board of Technical Education
  - Direct admission to 3rd Semester

1.2 Entrance Exam Requirements

  Mandatory Entrance Exams (any one):
  - JEE Main 2026: Score card with NTA percentile
  - Telangana State EAMCET 2026: Rank card
  - AP EAMCET 2026: Rank card

  Minimum Qualifying Marks for AITM:
  - JEE Main: No minimum cutoff, but preference given above 60 percentile
  - EAMCET: No minimum cutoff for state quota (75%) seats

1.3 Age Limit

  - Minimum age: 17 years as on December 31, 2026
  - Maximum age: No upper age limit for B.Tech admissions as per AICTE norms

SECTION 4: TRANSFER & WITHDRAWAL POLICY

4.1 Inter-Institute Transfer

  - Students may apply for transfer to another AICTE-approved institute within the first 30 days of the first semester.
  - Transfer certificate (TC) will be issued within 7 working days of application.
  - All dues must be cleared before TC is issued.
  - Caution deposit (₹10,000) will be refunded along with TC after dues clearance.

4.2 Program Change Request

  - Students may request a change of program within AITM within the first 15 days of the academic year.
  - Change is subject to seat availability in the target program.
  - No additional fee for program change if both programs have the same tuition fee.
  - If the new program has higher tuition, the difference must be paid immediately.

4.3 Re-Admission Policy

  - Students who leave voluntarily and wish to re-join must apply fresh through the standard admission process.
  - Academic credits from the previous enrollment may be transferred at the discretion of the Academic Council.

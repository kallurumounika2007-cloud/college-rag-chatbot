2.2 Mess Rules

SECTION 3: HOSTEL RULES & REGULATIONS

3.1 Entry & Exit (Curfew Policy)

  - All students MUST be inside the hostel campus by 9:30 PM every day (biometric check-in required at the main gate).
  - The hostel main gate closes at 10:00 PM. Late entry after 10:00 PM requires prior written permission from the Chief Warden.
  - Unauthorized late entry (after 10:30 PM without permission) will result in a disciplinary action (first offense: warning letter; second offense: suspension of hostel privileges for 1 month; third offense: expulsion from hostel).

3.2 Night-Out & Leave Policy

  - Students may apply for a night-out (staying outside campus) through the AITM Student Portal (portal.aitm.edu.in) with 24 hours advance notice.
  - Night-outs require BOTH parental consent (via registered mobile OTP) AND Warden approval.
  - Maximum allowed night-outs per semester: 5 (General students), 10 (students with medical or family emergency approval).
  - Students on night-out must return and check-in before 6:00 AM on the day of return.

3.3 Visitors Policy

  - Visitors (parents/guardians only) are permitted in the Hostel Visitor Lounge between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM on Sundays and public holidays.
  - Visitors are NOT allowed inside the hostel rooms or blocks under any circumstances.
  - Prior entry must be approved via the hostel guard on duty with a valid government photo ID.
  - Students are responsible for the behavior of their visitors on campus.

3.4 Prohibited Items & Activities

  Strictly PROHIBITED in hostel rooms and campus:
  - Alcohol, tobacco, gutka, or any form of intoxicants
  - Electric cooking appliances (hot plates, electric kettles, toasters, induction cookers)
  - Non-prescription drugs or narcotics
  - Weapons of any kind
  - Unauthorized guests or overnight stays by non-hostel students
  - Loud music or noise disturbance after 10:30 PM

  Consequences: Any student found with prohibited items will face immediate disciplinary action including potential expulsion from the hostel and a case lodged with the Anti-Ragging and Discipline Committee.

3.5 Room Inspection

  - Wardens and Hostel Staff conduct surprise room inspections at any time.
  - Students must maintain cleanliness. Rooms are rated monthly; students with consistently poor ratings will be fined ₹200/month.

SECTION 4: ANTI-RAGGING POLICY

4.1 Zero Tolerance Policy

  AITM has a ZERO TOLERANCE policy for ragging in any form. Ragging is defined as any act that causes physical or psychological harm, discomfort, embarrassment, or humiliation to any student.

4.2 Anti-Ragging Committee

  Chief Anti-Ragging Officer: Dr. Meenakshi Sundaram (Dean, Student Welfare)
  Student Grievance Helpline: 040-2345-6800 (24×7)
  National Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522 (Toll-free, 24×7)
  Email: antiragging@aitm.edu.in

4.3 Consequences of Ragging

  Any student found guilty of ragging will face:
  - Immediate suspension from the institute
  - Expulsion from hostel
  - FIR (First Information Report) filed with local police
  - Cancellation of scholarship
  - Dismissal from the institution without any fee refund

SECTION 2: SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAMS

2.1 Chairman's Merit Scholarship

  Eligibility Criteria:
  - Category A (100% Tuition Waiver): JEE Main rank 1-1000, OR State CET top 50 rank, OR 10+2 PCM aggregate above 98%
  - Category B (75% Tuition Waiver): JEE Main rank 1001-5000, OR 10+2 PCM aggregate 95%-97.9%
  - Category C (50% Tuition Waiver): JEE Main rank 5001-15000, OR 10+2 PCM aggregate 90%-94.9%

  Renewal Conditions: CGPA must be maintained above 8.5 each semester. Scholarship is cancelled if CGPA drops below 8.0.

2.2 Government & State Scholarships

  - Rajiv Gandhi Scholarship (SC/ST): Full fee reimbursement by state government. Students must apply via the state scholarship portal (scholarship.ap.gov.in) by October 15, 2026.
  - OBC Creamy Layer Waiver: 25% tuition waiver for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) students with family income below ₹8 lakh per annum.
  - EWS Scholarship: 10% tuition waiver for Economically Weaker Section students with family income below ₹2.5 lakh per annum.
  - PMO PRAGATI Scholarship: For girl students in technical programs. Amount: ₹50,000 per year. Apply via NFS portal.

2.3 Sports & Cultural Scholarships

  - National-level sports representatives: 100% tuition waiver for the duration of their program.
  - State-level sports representatives: 50% tuition waiver.
  - National cultural award winners: 25% tuition waiver.

2.4 Sibling & Alumni Discount

  - If two siblings are simultaneously enrolled at AITM, the second sibling receives a 10% tuition discount.
  - Children of AITM alumni receive a flat ₹10,000 discount on annual tuition.

SECTION 3: FEE PAYMENT SCHEDULE & POLICY

3.1 Payment Deadlines

  Semester        Payment Deadline        Late Fee
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Semester 1      August 15, 2026         ₹500/week after deadline
  Semester 2      January 15, 2027        ₹500/week after deadline

  Note: Students with unpaid fees beyond 30 days of the deadline will be barred from attending classes, examinations, and accessing the library until dues are cleared.

3.2 Payment Methods

  Fees can be paid via:
  - AITM Student Portal (portal.aitm.edu.in) — NEFT/UPI/Credit Card/Debit Card
  - SBI Challan at any SBI branch using Institution Code: 97234
  - DD in favour of "Principal, AITM" payable at Hyderabad

3.3 Fee Refund Policy

  Withdrawal Date                           Refund Amount
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Before commencement of classes            100% tuition (minus ₹5,000 processing)
  Within 15 days of class commencement      80% tuition
  Within 30 days of class commencement      60% tuition
  After 30 days from commencement           No refund

  Caution deposit (₹10,000) is fully refundable upon proper clearance at exit/graduation.

3.4 Education Loan Assistance

  - Canara Bank: Up to ₹15 lakh at 9.0% p.a.
  - HDFC Credila: Up to ₹40 lakh at market rate
  - Axis Bank: Up to ₹20 lakh with moratorium until 12 months after placement
